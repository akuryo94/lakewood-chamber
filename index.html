What the Chamber does

Four commitments guide everything we do, from monthly breakfasts to our work with City Hall.

Connect

Morning breakfasts, lunch roundtables and evening mixers hosted at Lakewood businesses.

Advocate

A steady voice for local business with the City of Lakewood on permits, zoning and growth.

Promote

A public member directory, Shop & Dine Lakewood campaigns and member spotlights.

Report openly

Board minutes, tax filings and financial statements published on this website.
