Transparency

Members and the public can review how the Chamber is governed and how its money is managed.

Board of Directors

Public documents

Approved Board minutes, IRS Form 990 filings and quarterly financial statements.

Financial controls

  • The Board Chair and Treasurer are always different people
  • Payments over $1,000 need approval from both the Treasurer and the Chair
  • An independent director reconciles bank statements every month
  • Documents are published within 30 days of Board approval
